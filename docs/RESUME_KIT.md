# Resume / freelance positioning

## Project title
**E-commerce Revenue & Customer Retention Intelligence Dashboard**

## CV bullets
- Built an end-to-end transaction analytics pipeline for 9,000+ messy e-commerce orders, covering validation, deduplication, refunds, discounts, profitability, cohorts, RFM segmentation, churn-risk scoring, and revenue forecasting.
- Converted raw order exports into executive KPIs, monthly performance trends, retention cohorts, product/channel profitability tables, and a prioritized commercial action memo.
- Identified customer groups for win-back campaigns and isolated discount leakage by category and channel rather than relying on average order value alone.
- Delivered a reproducible Python workflow, publication-ready charts, CSV exports, tests, and an interactive Streamlit dashboard.

## Freelance profile description
I turn messy sales and customer data into clear business decisions. I can clean transaction exports, build KPI reports, analyze retention and repeat purchases, segment customers with RFM, measure product and channel profitability, create forecasts, and deliver an interactive dashboard or Excel-ready output. My reports focus on what changed, why it changed, and what the business should do next.

## Proposal template
Hi, I reviewed your project and can turn the raw export into a decision-ready report rather than just a collection of charts. I will first validate and clean the data, document assumptions, then calculate revenue, margin, repeat-purchase, cohort-retention, customer-segment, product, channel, and regional KPIs. I will finish with a concise dashboard and prioritized recommendations tied to measurable actions. I can also provide the cleaned dataset and reproducible analysis files so the work is easy to update next month. Could you share the file format, reporting period, and the three decisions you want the analysis to support?
