# Client handoff checklist

1. Confirm definitions: completed order, refund, cancellation, revenue, cost, discount, active customer.
2. Confirm the reporting period and timezone.
3. Deliver cleaned data dictionary and validation summary.
4. Deliver KPI workbook / CSV exports and dashboard link or local run instructions.
5. Walk through three findings and three recommended actions.
6. Agree on the monthly refresh format and ownership.
