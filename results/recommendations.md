# Commercial recommendations

1. Protect the highest-profit product and channel combinations before increasing volume.
2. Build a win-back campaign for the At risk segment, prioritising customers with high monetary value.
3. Review categories with high average discount and below-average margin.
4. Use the cohort table to track whether new-customer retention improves month over month.
5. Treat the forecast as a planning baseline, not a promise; refresh it after each completed month.
